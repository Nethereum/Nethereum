# X402 Payments — Nethereum 7.1

Nethereum 7.1 takes `Nethereum.X402` from the 7.0 v2 wire format to a conformant, hardened x402 v2 stack. A route can offer several payment requirements (`RoutePaymentConfig.Accepts`) and the middleware verifies and settles only the offer the payer actually accepted; it can settle before the handler runs (`extra.paymentFlow = "upfront"`); a payer who holds only the token can pay through Permit2 with the `eip2612GasSponsoring` and `erc20ApprovalGasSponsoring` extensions; and an authorizer can cancel an unsettled EIP-3009 authorization with a signed `cancelAuthorization`. The facilitator gains `X402FacilitatorOptions` (gas ceiling, per-network asset allowlist, ASP.NET Core authorization policy, confirmations, route prefix), a bounded and serialised send path, front-run recovery that is bound to the payment, and ERC-1271 smart-wallet settlement through the `bytes` signature overloads of EIP-3009 tokens. Six security rounds during the work and a nine-round audit before release close replay-key, route-matching, canonical-form, cache and denial-of-service holes, each with a test that fails before its fix, and error reasons follow the x402 reference. No public member was removed or renamed, but several error-code strings, HTTP statuses and validation rules changed; read Breaking Changes before upgrading.

## Nethereum.X402 — Routes With Several Offers, and Route Matching

A route is no longer a single requirement. The middleware answers a request that carries a payment by finding the offer whose JSON deep-equals `PaymentPayload.Accepted`, and settles that offer only.

- `RoutePaymentConfig.Accepts` (`List<PaymentRequirements>?`), `GetOffers()` (the `Accepts` list, else `Requirements` as one offer) and `Extensions` (`Dictionary<string, object>?`, served in `PaymentRequired.Extensions`)
- `X402FacilitatorProxyProcessor.FindMatchingRequirements(RoutePaymentConfig route, PaymentPayload payment)`; a payment whose `accepted` matches no offer gets 402 `X402ErrorMessages.NoMatchingPaymentRequirements` ("No matching payment requirements") and the facilitator is never called
- `PaymentRequirements.ExtensionData` and `ResourceInfo.ExtensionData` (`[JsonExtensionData]`) keep unknown fields, so a reference client's echo still matches
- Route patterns follow the reference `parseRoutePattern`: a trailing `/*` also covers the bare prefix, repeated and trailing slashes are ignored, `:name` and `[name]` are single-segment parameters, matching is culture-invariant, case-insensitive and non-backtracking, and the most specific pattern wins (literal over parameter over wildcard segments, then declaration order). Backslash, percent-encoded slash and trailing-dot spellings of a protected path are protected too
- `X402Options.Validate` accepts the bare `*` pattern (every path) and rejects any other pattern that does not start with `/` or that carries `?`, `#` or `://`

## Nethereum.X402 — Upfront Payment Flow

A route can settle before its handler runs, for work that cannot be undone.

- `X402PaymentFlows.Authorization` / `X402PaymentFlows.Upfront` and `ExactSchemeExtra.PaymentFlow` (`extra.paymentFlow`); omitting it means `authorization`
- On an upfront route the middleware calls only the facilitator's settle, runs the handler after a successful settlement, and every handler response carries `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` and a `Cache-Control` that includes `private`; a failed or throwing settlement returns 402 with `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` and the handler does not run
- `X402RequirementSupport.IsAuthorizationFlow`, `IsUpfrontFlow`, `ResolveAssetTransferMethod` and `IsSupportedByThisLibrary`; the default selector skips requirements this library cannot sign and prefers the `authorization` flow when both are offered
- `X402Options.Validate` rejects a route whose `paymentFlow` is neither `authorization` nor `upfront`

## Nethereum.X402 — Permit2 Gas Sponsoring

A payer who has never approved Permit2 can still pay: the facilitator covers the approval.

- `X402GasSponsoringExtensions.DeclareEip2612()` and `DeclareErc20Approval()` build the server-side declarations for `RoutePaymentConfig.Extensions`; `X402ExtensionKeys.Eip2612GasSponsoring` and `Erc20ApprovalGasSponsoring` are the keys
- `Eip2612GasSponsoringInfo` and `Erc20ApprovalGasSponsoringInfo` are the `info` objects the client attaches; `Eip2612PermitSigner` (`GetTypedData`, `Sign`, `RecoverSigner`) signs and recovers the EIP-2612 permit; `SettleWithPermitFunction`, `Eip2612PermitParams`, `Eip2612NoncesFunction` and `Permit2AuthorizationMapper.ToSettleWithPermitFunction` settle through `x402ExactPermit2Proxy.settleWithPermit`
- `Erc20ApprovalGasSponsoringOptions` (`Enabled`, `MaxSponsoredGasCost`, `MinimumPermitDeadlineSeconds` = 120, `Settlement`, `Validate()`) is the facilitator's opt-in, passed to `X402ExactPermit2Service`; the default `Erc20ApprovalSponsoredSettlement` first tops up the payer's gas shortfall with a facilitator transfer and waits for it (`gas_funding_failed`, or `settlement_pending` if the receipt never comes), then relays the approval and waits for its receipt, then settles; implement `IErc20ApprovalSponsoredSettlement` (it receives an `Erc20ApprovalSponsoredSettlementRequest`) to replace it with an atomic sender
- A sponsored approval is bounded at verify, before any funding: EIP-1559 only, low-s signature, gas limit at most the smaller of the block gas limit and 16777216 and at most the larger of 70000 and 1.5 times the estimate, priority fee between 1 wei and the larger of twice the node's suggestion and 0.1 gwei, max fee at least 1.125 times the base fee and at most the larger of 3 times the base fee plus the priority fee and 1 gwei; the same payer and nonce cannot be relayed twice concurrently (`erc20_approval_tx_in_flight`)
- `X402HttpClientOptions.RpcClientsByChainId` (`Dictionary<int, IClient>?`) lets the client sign the sponsoring info when the server declared the extension and the payer's Permit2 allowance is below the amount; `CapSponsoredApprovalToPaymentAmount` (default `false`, approve `uint256` max) limits the sponsored approval to the payment amount; the client signs the priority fee as the node's suggestion clamped to between 1 wei and 0.1 gwei
- `X402ExactSchemeProcessor.GetSupportedAsync` and `SupportedPaymentKindsResponse` now list `Extensions` and `Signers` (`SupportedPaymentKindsResponse.Extensions`, `Signers`), each kind once with no null fields

## Nethereum.X402 — Facilitator Guards and Hosting

`X402FacilitatorOptions` carries the guards every facilitator transaction passes through; register it with `AddX402FacilitatorOptions` and pass it to the processors.

- `X402FacilitatorOptions`: `MaxSettlementGas` (500000), `AllowedAssets` (per canonical `eip155:<chainId>`), `FacilitatorAddresses`, `RoutePrefix` (`"facilitator"`, `""` for the root), `AuthorizationPolicy`, `RequiredConfirmations` (1), `MaxReceiptWaitSeconds` (3600), `Validate()`, `IsAssetAllowed(network, asset)`; `ServiceCollectionExtensionsFacilitator.AddX402FacilitatorOptions(Action<X402FacilitatorOptions>)`
- `X402TransferWithAuthorisation3009Service`, `X402ReceiveWithAuthorisation3009Service` and `X402ExactPermit2Service` gain constructors that take `X402FacilitatorOptions`; the `AddX402TransferProcessor`, `AddX402ExactProcessor` and `AddX402ReceiveProcessor` registrations use the registered options
- A settlement whose gas estimate is above `MaxSettlementGas` is refused with `settlement_gas_exceeds_limit` and nothing is sent; transactions are sent with an explicit gas limit of 120% of the estimate, never above the ceiling, and serialised per account and chain across the process
- A settlement that is broadcast but has no receipt inside its bound returns `settlement_pending` with the hash (`X402SettlementPendingException`); a settle that reverts because a third party already submitted the payer's signed payment reports success with that transaction when the chain proves it belongs to this payment (EIP-3009 `AuthorizationUsed` followed by the matching `Transfer`; Permit2 a `settle` or `settleWithPermit` call to the proxy with this owner, token, nonce, amount and recipient), after the required confirmations, once per process, and never for the facilitator's own transaction
- Settlement succeeds only when the receipt holds exactly one matching `Transfer` (`invalid_exact_evm_transfer_event_mismatch` otherwise)
- The hosted `FacilitatorController` mounts under `RoutePrefix` and, when `AuthorizationPolicy` names a registered ASP.NET Core policy, answers 401 to an unauthenticated caller and 403 to one that fails the policy
- `HttpFacilitatorClient` bounds verify (`VerifyTimeout`, 30 s) and settle (`SettleTimeoutGrace` plus three times the settlement bound, where the bound is the requirement's `MaxTimeoutSeconds` clamped to 1 to 3600 seconds, 60 s when it is 0), returns the failed `VerificationResponse` or `SettlementResponse` carried in a non-2xx body, sends `x402Version` in the request body (`FacilitatorVerifyRequest.X402Version`, `FacilitatorSettleRequest.X402Version`), and is registered under `HttpFacilitatorClient.HttpClientName` with an infinite `HttpClient.Timeout` so the bound governs
- `VerificationResponse.InvalidMessage`, `VerificationResponse.Extensions`, `VerificationResponse.Extra` and `SettlementResponse.ErrorMessage` carry a readable cause; `Payer` is omitted from the JSON when null

## Nethereum.X402 — EIP-3009 Conformance, Smart Wallets and Cancellation

The EIP-3009 processors follow the reference reasons and settle contract-wallet payments.

- Verify simulates the settlement and reports the reference reasons: `invalid_exact_evm_missing_eip712_domain`, `invalid_exact_evm_eip3009_not_supported`, `invalid_exact_evm_payload_undeployed_smart_wallet`, and, after a failed simulation, `invalid_exact_evm_token_name_mismatch` / `invalid_exact_evm_token_version_mismatch` from the token's on-chain `name()` / `version()`; a malformed signature is a signature error, never `unexpected_verify_error`
- A signature that is not 65 bytes is valid only as an ERC-1271 signature and settles through `TransferWithAuthorizationBytesSignatureFunction` / `ReceiveWithAuthorizationBytesSignatureFunction` (`ExactScheme3009ServiceBase.SubmitContractSignatureSettlementAsync`, `EstimateContractSignatureSettlementGasAsync`)
- `X402TransferWithAuthorisation3009Service.CancelAuthorizationAsync(authorizerAddress, nonce, authorizerSignature, network, tokenAddress, cancellationToken)` cancels an unsettled authorization with the authorizer's EIP-712 signature; `CancelAuthorisationBuilder.GetTypedDataForCancellation`, `TransferWithAuthorisationSigner.SignCancelWithPrivateKeyAsync` and `SignCancelWithWeb3Async` produce it
- `Eip3009AuthorisationBuilderBase.BuildFromPaymentRequirements` defaults `validBefore` to now plus `MaxTimeoutSeconds` (one hour when it is 0)
- A `CancelAuthorizationAsync` whose `CancellationToken` is cancelled while another send holds the facilitator's per-account lock throws before anything is broadcast instead of waiting behind that settlement
- `PaymentAuthorizationValidator` and `ValidationResult.BlockNumber` expose the block the funds, nonce and time checks ran against

## Nethereum.X402 — Client

`X402HttpClient` echoes what the server sent and signs only what it can pay.

- The signed `PaymentPayload` echoes the 402's `Extensions` and `Resource` and keeps unknown fields of the accepted requirement; unpadded base64 is accepted for `PAYMENT-REQUIRED`, `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` and `PAYMENT-RESPONSE`
- `X402HttpClientOptions.MaxAuthorizationValiditySeconds` (`DefaultMaxAuthorizationValiditySeconds` = 3600) caps the EIP-3009 `validBefore` and the Permit2 `deadline` at the smaller of the requirement's `MaxTimeoutSeconds` and the cap; the manual flow is capped too
- A requirement that is not `exact` with an `authorization` or `upfront` flow and an `eip3009` or `permit2` method is skipped by `DefaultPaymentRequirementsSelector` and refused by the signing path with `InvalidOperationException`
- `TransferWithAuthorisationSigner.SignWithWeb3Async`, `SignReceiveWithWeb3Async` and `SignCancelWithWeb3Async` sign through a Web3 account (see Bug Fixes)

## Nethereum.X402 — Server Hardening

- Replay protection: `PaymentReplayIdentity.Extract(PaymentPayload, PaymentRequirements)` derives the key from the typed payload as `network:method:asset:payer:nonce` with a canonical payer and nonce; a payment without one is rejected with 400 `invalid_payload`. `IPaymentReplayStore.ReleaseAsync` frees a reservation that did not end in a settled or pending payment (handler status 400 or above, a failed settlement, an exception or a cancelled request; an upfront payment that fails to settle). `InMemoryPaymentReplayStore(int maxEntries)`, `MaxEntries` and `DefaultMaxEntries` (100000) bound the default store; a full store throws `PaymentReplayStoreFullException` and the middleware answers 503 `replay_store_full` without running the endpoint
- Responses: every 402 carries `Cache-Control: no-store`; a settled response drops `public` and `s-maxage`, replaces a qualified `private="..."` with a bare `private`, and adds `private`; the 402 from a failed settlement carries `PAYMENT-RESPONSE`
- `X402Options.Validate` holds `ResourceInfo` to the v2 limits (`ServiceName` and each tag printable ASCII of at most 32 characters, at most five tags, `IconUrl` an absolute http or https URL of at most 2048 characters) and requires `PaymentReplayRetention` greater than zero while replay protection is on
- `ResourceInfo.ServiceName`, `Tags` and `IconUrl` are served in the 402
- Untrusted numbers are plain ASCII digits capped at 78 digits, EIP-3009 nonces must be exactly 32 bytes, and `Caip2.TryParseEip155ChainId` accepts only the canonical `eip155:<chainId>` spelling, so a non-canonical network or nonce cannot bypass the replay key or the asset allowlist
- `X402FacilitatorOptions.Validate` rejects an `AllowedAssets` key that is not a canonical `eip155` CAIP-2 network, so a mistyped key fails at registration instead of leaving the network unrestricted
- A replay retention too large to add to the current time keeps the reservation until it is evicted instead of throwing on every paid request

## Nethereum.X402 — Permit2 Revert Reasons

- `X402ErrorCodes.Permit2InvalidNonce`, `Permit2InvalidAmount`, `Permit2InvalidDestination`, `Permit2InvalidOwner`, `Permit2PaymentTooEarly` and `Permit2612AmountMismatch`, decoded from the proxy's and Permit2's custom errors by `Permit2SettlementErrorMapper.MapReason` (`InvalidDestinationError`, `InvalidOwnerError`, `PaymentTooEarlyError`, `Permit2612AmountMismatchError`)
- The Permit2 verify steps run in the specification's order and report `permit2_allowance_required`, `permit2_insufficient_balance`, `permit2_amount_mismatch`, `permit2_deadline_expired`, `permit2_not_yet_valid`, `permit2_token_mismatch` and `permit2_proxy_not_deployed` accordingly

## Nethereum.X402 — Replay, Pending Settlements and Bounded Bodies

- The replay identity comes from the transfer method of the requirements the payment matched. Against EIP-3009 requirements, `X402ExactSchemeProcessor` rejects a payload that also carries a `permit2Authorization`.
- On an upfront route the middleware verifies before it reserves the payment, and keeps the reservation while settlement is pending.
- A settle whose outcome is unknown (the facilitator call threw, timed out, or answered without a definite status and reason) keeps the reservation and answers `settlement_pending`. Only a definite failure releases it. A call counts as provably unsent only on an `HttpRequestException` with `ConnectionError` or `NameResolutionError`, and a node's RPC rejection counts as definite unless it says the transaction is already known.
- The facilitator's verify, settle and supported responses and the client's 402 body are read with a 1 MiB limit (`HttpFacilitatorClient.MaxResponseBytes`, `X402HttpClientOptions.MaxResponseBytes`, default `X402HttpClientOptions.DefaultMaxResponseBytes`). The automatic client's first exchange stays within `HttpClient.Timeout`.
- JSON with a duplicated property name is rejected and never matches a requirement: case-insensitively on typed objects, by exact name in `extensions` and `extra`.
- `ExactSchemeExtra.FromRequirements` and `X402RequirementSupport` no longer throw on hostile input, and `X402ExactSchemeProcessor` chooses the transfer path from the requirements.

## Bug Fixes

- **EIP-3009 signatures whose `r` or `s` has a leading zero byte (about 1 in 128) are recovered and settled correctly.** Recovery rebuilt the signature from the unpadded `r` and `s`, mis-recovering the payer, and settlement passed them as `bytes32`, which ABI encoding pads on the right, so the token received a different signature. Recovery now uses `CreateStringSignature` and settlement left-pads `r` and `s` to 32 bytes
- **`TransferWithAuthorisationSigner.SignWithWeb3Async`, `SignReceiveWithWeb3Async` and `SignCancelWithWeb3Async` work.** They sent the signer address as the `eth_signTypedData_v4` message and the typed data as the request id, so every call failed; they now send the typed data in Nethereum's EIP-712 JSON and refuse a signer address that is not the Web3 account (`ArgumentException`)
- **`CancelAuthorizationAsync` can cancel.** The 7.0 overload sent a zero signature, which a conformant token rejects; it is now `[Obsolete]` and the overload that takes the authorizer's signature is the working one
- **A reserved payment no longer stays reserved after it failed.** The reservation was never released, so a payment whose handler returned 404 or whose settlement failed could not be retried and, repeated, filled the store; it is released as described under Server Hardening
- **Replay-key bypasses.** The key was read case-insensitively from raw JSON (a decoy `authorization` property before the real one, a `0x`-less nonce or payer spelling gave another key); it is now derived from the typed payload with a canonical payer and nonce
- **Route bypasses.** A trailing-dot, backslash or encoded-slash spelling of a protected path reached the endpoint unpaid, and `/api/data/*` did not cover `/api/data`
- **Asset-allowlist and replay bypass through non-canonical spellings** of a network (`eip155:084532`) or an EIP-3009 nonce (shorter or longer than 32 bytes)
- **Denial of service by huge numbers.** A million-digit amount, nonce or extension number made `BigInteger.Parse` run for seconds; such input is now `invalid_payload` in under a second
- **Facilitator `/supported`** listed a kind once per processor and emitted null fields; each version, scheme and network now appears once
- **Shared caches could store a paid response.** `public`, `s-maxage` and a qualified `private="..."` are removed from a settled response
- **EIP-3009 verify mapped malformed payloads to `unexpected_verify_error`**; missing fields, non-numeric fields and values that are not plain ASCII digits are `invalid_payload`

## Breaking Changes

No public type or member was removed or renamed. The following change behaviour that callers may match on or depend on.

Error-code strings (`VerificationResponse.InvalidReason`, `SettlementResponse.ErrorReason`, the 402 `error`):

- An EIP-3009 settlement transaction that is mined and reverted is `invalid_exact_evm_transaction_failed` (`X402ErrorCodes.TransactionFailed`); it was `invalid_transaction_state`. Permit2 and `CancelAuthorizationAsync` keep `invalid_transaction_state`
- A non-`exact` scheme is `invalid_exact_evm_scheme` (`X402ErrorCodes.InvalidScheme`); it was `unsupported_payload_type`, which now means only that the requirement's asset transfer method is not the processor's
- `X402ErrorCodes.UnsupportedScheme` is now `"unsupported_scheme"` (it was `"unsupported_payload_type"`) and the library no longer returns it; `X402ErrorCodes.AuthorizationValueMismatch` is new, defined and never returned. A value that differs from the required amount is still `invalid_exact_evm_authorization_value` (`X402ErrorCodes.InvalidValue`)
- `extra` without `name` or `version` on the EIP-3009 path is `invalid_exact_evm_missing_eip712_domain` (`X402ErrorCodes.MissingEip712Domain`); it was `invalid_payment_requirements`
- A Permit2 `InvalidNonce` revert is `permit2_invalid_nonce` (was `invalid_exact_evm_nonce_already_used`); a Permit2 or proxy `InvalidAmount` revert is `permit2_invalid_amount` (was `permit2_amount_mismatch`). Verify still reports `invalid_exact_evm_nonce_already_used` for a used nonce and `permit2_amount_mismatch` for an amount that differs from the requirement
- New reasons a caller may now receive: `settlement_pending`, `settlement_gas_exceeds_limit`, `invalid_exact_evm_asset_not_allowed`, `invalid_exact_evm_transfer_event_mismatch`, `replay_store_full`, `gas_funding_failed`, `invalid_eip2612_extension_format`, `invalid_erc20_approval_extension_format`, `invalid_exact_evm_eip3009_not_supported`, the `permit2_invalid_*` and `permit2_payment_too_early` codes, and the `eip2612_*` and `erc20_approval_*` codes listed in the README
- EIP-3009 verify now also simulates the settlement, so a payment that passed 7.0 verify and failed at settle is now rejected at verify with `invalid_exact_evm_transaction_simulation_failed` (or a token name or version mismatch)
- A malformed number, nonce or signature is `invalid_payload` or `invalid_exact_evm_signature`, where 7.0 returned `unexpected_verify_error`

HTTP behaviour of `X402Middleware`:

- A `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` header that does not decode, or a verification that reports `invalid_payload`, is 400 (was 402); `permit2_allowance_required` is 412; a full replay store is 503; every 402 carries `Cache-Control: no-store`
- A failed or throwing settlement is 402 with the `SettlementResponse` in `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` and the body `{}`; it no longer carries `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` or a `PaymentRequired` body. Read the reason with `HttpResponseMessage.GetPaymentError()`
- A payment whose `accepted` does not equal an offer of the route gets 402 "No matching payment requirements"; a client must echo the requirement it was offered
- With replay protection on, a payment from which no identity can be derived is rejected with 400 `invalid_payload` (7.0 logged a warning and skipped the reservation), and a shared `IPaymentReplayStore` sees a new key shape (`network:method:asset:payer:nonce`)
- `IPaymentReplayStore.ReleaseAsync` is a default interface method that does nothing: a custom store must implement it for released reservations to be freed before `PaymentReplayRetention` expires
- A settled response loses `public` and `s-maxage` and gains `private` in its `Cache-Control`

Validation that now rejects what 7.0 accepted:

- The private-key overloads of `AddX402TransferProcessor` and `AddX402ReceiveProcessor` build the `Account` when you register, so an invalid key throws at registration instead of at the first resolve of `IX402PaymentProcessor` (the `AddX402ExactProcessor` private-key overload already did)
- `X402Options.Validate` (and so `UseX402` and the `X402Middleware` constructor) throws for a route pattern that does not start with `/` (other than `*`) or carries `?`, `#` or `://`, for a `ResourceInfo` over the v2 limits, for an unsupported `paymentFlow`, for a null entry in `Accepts`, and for `PaymentReplayRetention` of zero or less while replay protection is on
- Route matching changed: `/api/data/*` now also matches `/api/data`, repeated and trailing slashes are ignored, `:name` and `[name]` segments are parameters, and the order of overlapping routes is by specificity (7.0: patterns without a wildcard first, then the longer pattern)
- `X402HttpClientOptions.Validate` throws when `MaxAuthorizationValiditySeconds` is zero or less; the client now signs `validBefore` and the Permit2 `deadline` as now plus the smaller of `MaxTimeoutSeconds` and that cap (7.0: one hour for EIP-3009 whatever the requirement said, and the requirement's timeout uncapped for Permit2)
- `X402FacilitatorOptions.Validate` throws for a non-positive `MaxSettlementGas`, `RequiredConfirmations` below 1, `MaxReceiptWaitSeconds` below 1, a null `RoutePrefix` and an `AllowedAssets` key that is not a canonical `eip155:<chainId>`
- `Caip2.TryParseEip155ChainId` returns `false` for a reference with a leading zero, a non-digit or more than 78 characters; EIP-3009 verify rejects a nonce that is not `0x` plus 64 hex characters; numbers must be plain ASCII digits of at most 78 characters
- A facilitator refuses a settlement whose gas estimate is above `X402FacilitatorOptions.MaxSettlementGas` (500000 by default), and settlement succeeds only with exactly one matching `Transfer` in the receipt, so a fee-on-transfer token that was settled before is now `invalid_exact_evm_transfer_event_mismatch`
- `X402HttpClient` refuses to sign a requirement it does not support (`InvalidOperationException`) and its selector skips one

- A JSON body with a duplicated property name is rejected, and so is an EIP-3009 payment that also carries a `permit2Authorization`; a facilitator or 402 body over 1 MiB fails
- A settle whose outcome is unknown keeps the replay reservation and answers `settlement_pending` instead of releasing it

API surface and wire shape:

- `X402TransferWithAuthorisation3009Service.CancelAuthorizationAsync(authorizerAddress, nonce, network, tokenAddress, cancellationToken)` is `[Obsolete]` (a warning, an error under warnings-as-errors); use the overload that takes `authorizerSignature`
- `SettlementResponse.Transaction` defaults to `""` (was `null!`), and `Payer` is omitted from the JSON when null; `ExactSchemeExtra` omits a null `name` or `version`; `PaymentReplayIdentity.Extract(PaymentPayload)` returns `string?` and reads only typed payloads
- `HttpFacilitatorClient` no longer throws `HttpRequestException` for a non-2xx answer that carries a verification or settlement body; it returns the failed response. Verify that exceeds `VerifyTimeout` throws `TimeoutException`; settle that exceeds its bound returns `unexpected_settle_error`
- `AddX402Services` and `AddX402FacilitatorProxy` resolve the facilitator `HttpClient` from the named client `HttpFacilitatorClient.HttpClientName` with an infinite timeout instead of the unnamed default client
- `AddX402FacilitatorControllers` also registers the route-prefix convention; the hosted endpoints stay under `/facilitator` unless `RoutePrefix` is set
